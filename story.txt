The Mehlhase led us down the Hauptstrasse to a magnificent schloss.
We ended the night in a cozy Biergarten, enjoying Brot and Bratwurst while listening to Rammstein.
With a fresh start to the next day, I put on new pair of lederhose, leaving the klimaanlage and returning to the hauptstrasse.
